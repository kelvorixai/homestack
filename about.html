About HomeStack

A small library of plain-English, data-driven guides on what renting and buying property in the UAE actually costs — deposits, fees, registration costs, and the breakeven math between the two.

Most property content online is written to help you pick a neighborhood or decide whether to buy. HomeStack deliberately isn't that. Every guide here breaks down the mechanics behind a cost figure — what actually sits on top of the advertised rent, how a rent-vs-buy breakeven is actually calculated — using the underlying fee structures and methodology, not a recommendation.

Everything here is general education, not personalized financial or real estate advice, and not tied to any developer, agency, bank, or landlord. Figures are illustrative and based on commonly cited UAE market practice at the time of writing — real fees, rates, and deposits vary by emirate, building, and provider. For a number specific to your situation, confirm directly with your agent, landlord, bank, or the relevant land department.
