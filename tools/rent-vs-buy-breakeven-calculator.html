How this is calculated

This models the cumulative cash cost of each path year by year:

  • Buying's upfront cost: down payment + DLD transfer fee (4%) + agency commission (2%) + mortgage registration fee (0.25% of the loan)
  • Buying's ongoing "lost" cost: mortgage interest paid each year (not principal — that builds equity) plus service charges and maintenance
  • Renting's cost: cumulative rent, growing each year at your entered rate

The breakeven point is the first year cumulative buying cost drops at or below cumulative rent. Property value appreciation isn't included, since it's the least predictable input and applies to both a sold property and reinvested rent savings.

This is a projection based on the numbers you enter, not a quote — actual fees, rates, and costs vary. It doesn't account for taxes, opportunity cost of the down payment, or property appreciation.

Frequently asked questions

What upfront costs does this calculator include for buying?

The down payment, plus Dubai's DLD transfer fee (4% of the property price), agency commission (2%), and a mortgage registration fee (0.25% of the loan amount).

Why doesn't the breakeven point include property appreciation?

Because appreciation is the least predictable input, and it applies fairly evenly to both paths — a sold property's gain and money saved by renting and reinvested elsewhere both benefit from market growth, so leaving it out keeps the comparison focused on knowable cash costs.

Does mortgage principal count as a buying cost?

No — only the interest portion of each mortgage payment counts as a cost here, since principal repayment builds equity you keep, not money that's lost the way rent is.

What counts as renting's cost in this comparison?

Cumulative rent paid over your holding period, increasing each year at the annual rent growth rate you enter.
